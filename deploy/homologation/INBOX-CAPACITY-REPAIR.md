# Durable inbox capacity backpressure

Confirmed failure path: inbox.append throws INBOX_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED; transport flush emitted failure and closed AMQP; facade forwarded error to the business session; session cleanup stopped its inbox replay. Capacity therefore disabled the consumer needed to free that capacity. The reported five receipts, zero durable ACKs and inactive consumer match this path; no AWS log access was performed in this workspace.

Capacity rejection now retains the bounded, uncommitted batch without ACK, cancels only the broker consumer and keeps the transport actor, SQLite and local replay alive. Deliveries racing cancellation are individually requeued without ACK. When durable business checkpoints retire inbox rows, the actor retries the held transaction, ACKs only after FULL SQLite commit, and resumes the exclusive manual-ACK consumer with hysteresis (at most 8 MiB free, scaled down for small tests). It never purges the broker or deletes unprocessed rows. Invalid oversized messages remain terminal, not an endless capacity wait. Existing per-item, batch, prefetch, RAM delivery window and 2 GiB logical inbox bounds remain unchanged.

Snapshot recovery waits for capacity relief before HTTP/application. If capacity fills during a full snapshot apply, the application barrier temporarily permits bounded replay/checkpoint progress and resumes the snapshot afterward. This avoids snapshot/application/retirement circular waiting. Snapshot/live version ordering and blocked stale/incomplete quotes are preserved. Periodic publications no longer schedule after abort; explicit final checkpoint writes remain mandatory. The scoped six-GiB upgrade observes shutdown for at most ten minutes with existing inactivity, identity and recovery-proof checks intact, rather than repeatedly exhausting the known insufficient three-minute window.

Local evidence is in INBOX-CAPACITY-LOCAL-RESULTS.json. Real RabbitMQ in an isolated network, manual durable ACK, full restored inbox, 72/s input and 300-message burst: all 780 seeded/new messages uniquely processed and checkpointed; raw backlog 120 to zero with actual timestamped metric windows and consumer restored. A separate session test toggles capacity at startup and during apply, proving replay/checkpoint can unblock both waits. A scaled concurrent snapshot test restores 12000 inbox messages, accepts 660 more, applies/persists/restores 500 snapshot fixtures and ends with zero backlog. It is not production or 30-minute stability proof.

Deploy through apply-pipeline-recovery.mjs from the immutable candidate archive. It audits Mac absence, verifies current six-GiB identity/overrides, preserves current data in a new protected backup, and tests both the saturated-inbox proof and full-sized five-minute snapshot/load gate before stopping the current worker. Data-specific recovery checks remain protected and resumable. Prior migration and disk expansion are not repeated; no old finalized container is resumed. The new candidate must pass activation and actual feed validation before integration is declared recovered. Any capacity, disk, memory, source identity or checkpoint integrity failure stops with a diagnostic.

InPlay 31/32-only coverage remains separate. A broker consumer/ACK counter cannot satisfy market, snapshot, backlog-drain or odds freshness criteria.
